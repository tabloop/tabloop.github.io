Tabloop

Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Tabloop (tabloop.github.io) is a free web app for practising music: it plays song packages (.feedpak files) with their tab, and lets you slow down, loop, record yourself and track your practice. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it is kept and who can see it.

In short: Tabloop has no server and no user accounts. Your songs, recordings and microphone audio never leave your device. Your settings and practice history are stored in your browser, and only go elsewhere if you choose to back them up to your own Google Drive or sync them through a folder you pick. The hosted site sends anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Data stored on your device

Everything below is kept in your browser's own storage (local storage and IndexedDB) on your device, for this site only. Tabloop's developers cannot see it.

  • Songs you open: read from your device to play them; not uploaded anywhere.
  • Settings and practice history: your preferences (speed, colours, loops, playlists, tags, per-song settings) and how much you have practised.
  • Recorded takes: if you use ⏺ Rec, the audio you record is stored in your browser only. Takes are never included in exports, folder sync or Google Drive backups.
  • Microphone audio: with ⏺ Rec, 🎸 Tuner or 👂 Live, the app listens to your input to show your notes. The audio is processed on your device and not sent anywhere. The browser asks for your permission first, and the microphone is released when you turn these off.

You can export, import or delete your settings in ⚙ Settings, and remove everything by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose ⚙ Settings → Google Drive → Connect, Tabloop asks Google for permission to use its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). This is the only Google permission Tabloop asks for.

What Tabloop accessesOnly a private folder in your Drive that only Tabloop can see. It cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files, and it does not access your email, contacts or profile.
What it stores thereOne backup file with your Tabloop settings and practice history (the same information as Export settings), plus what's needed to merge backups from several browsers: when each setting and saved loop last changed, and for each browser that backs up a random identifier, a name such as “Chrome on macOS” (browser and operating system only) and that browser's own share of the practice history. No songs, no recordings, nothing that identifies you or your device beyond that.
WhySo you can restore your settings and history on another browser or computer, and keep several browsers in step: when Tabloop starts while signed in, it compares this browser with the backup.
Who can see itOnly you, through your Google account. The backup goes directly from your browser to Google; it does not pass through any Tabloop server (there is none), and Tabloop's developers never receive it.
How longUntil you delete it. The sign-in token lasts about an hour and is kept only in that browser tab.
How to remove itClick Disconnect in Tabloop's Settings. To delete the backup, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find Tabloop and choose Delete hidden app data. To revoke Tabloop's access, go to Google Account → Third-party apps & services.

Google API Services: Tabloop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google Drive is used only to provide the backup and restore feature to you; it is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to others, not used to train AI models, and not read by people.

Sync folder (optional)

If you choose a sync folder in Settings, Tabloop writes your settings and practice history to that folder on your computer. If the folder is synced by a service such as Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, that service's own privacy policy applies to the copy it syncs.

Anonymous usage counts

The hosted site (tabloop.github.io) sends anonymous counts to GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, so we know roughly how many people use Tabloop and which features are used. They include: that the app was opened, a song was opened or played, a loop was saved or a take recorded, and whether this browser is new or active today / this week. Songs are counted only by an anonymous ID, never by title or file name (unless you explicitly tick Also share the names of songs I play in Settings).

  • No cookies, no user ID, no third-party script; each count is a single small request.
  • No settings, practice history, recordings or Google data are ever sent.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected, and you can turn the counts off in ⚙ Settings → Send anonymous usage counts.
  • Copies of the app opened from a file on your computer never send counts.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load it, GitHub receives standard web request information (such as your IP address) as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The demo song is downloaded from the same site.

Children

Tabloop does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
