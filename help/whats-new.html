What's new in Tabloop

The latest first: what you can do now that you couldn't before, and what was fixed. The website always has the newest version.

Open TabloopHelp & guide

0.63.0 13 November 2026

Your own tags

  • 🏷 Tags: make your own labels for songs ("Gig set", "Warm-ups"…), with a colour, and put as many as you like on a song: the chips under the song's title, ＋ Tag to add one. 📖 Learning and ⭐ Mastered are still there as built-in tags.
  • Songs → 🏷 Tags lists them with their songs: rename, recolour, delete, or ▶ play a tag's songs in a row.
  • 📚 Library → Tags groups your songs by tag, with filters, and a 🏷 on each song to tag it without opening it.
  • Free: up to 3 tags of your own; ⭐ Premium: as many as you like. Tags are kept in your backups and synced like playlists.

0.62.0 13 November 2026

Loop over a backing track

  • ➿ Loop over this on a backing track (or New… → Over a backing track in Train's Looper): the band's chorus becomes the Looper's first layer, at its tempo and length, and you record your own layers over it. You can mute it or set its level; it can't be recorded over by mistake. 🥁 Remove bed takes it out.
  • Coming back to Train shows its tiles again when the Looper isn't playing (it's saved and closed when you leave); a Looper that's playing keeps going.

0.61.0 13 November 2026

The Looper is in Train

  • ➿ Looper moved from Create to Train, next to Scales, Ear training and Backing tracks. Its tile shows your saved session: ▶ Continue picks it up, New… starts fresh, and ‹ Train (or Esc) takes you back to the tiles.
  • Home's Train card has it too; Create keeps a link to it for now.

0.60.0 13 November 2026

Your takes in the Library

  • The 📚 Library window has a Takes tab after Loops: every recorded take of your library's songs, newest first, with its date, length and speed. Click one to open the song and play that take. The search finds takes by name or song.

0.59.2 13 November 2026

Play's pages in a new order

  • Play's pages are now Play along · Sections · Loop · Takes.

0.59.1 13 November 2026

Fold and move the Live box

  • 👂 Live's box over the tab folds to one row (just this pass's score) with its – button or a click on its top row; a click opens it again.
  • Drag it anywhere over the tab (by its dots or the box itself); double-click the dots to put it back in the corner. Where you put it and whether it's folded are remembered.

0.59.0 13 November 2026

Tags and loops in the Library

  • The 📚 Library window has two new tabs: Tags (your songs under 📖 Learning, ⭐ Mastered and Not tagged) and Loops (every song's saved loops; click one to open the song and loop it). The search works in both.
  • Home has 🕘 Recent between Library and YouTube, for the songs you played last.

0.58.1 13 November 2026

Straight to Play along

  • Opening a song takes you to Play → Play along, where 👂 Live and the song's stats are.
  • A message over the welcome screen (e.g. a song that couldn't be opened) now shows on top of the cards instead of behind them.

0.58.0 12 November 2026

Better with distortion

  • Notes played through distortion, fuzz or overdrive are recognised more often: when the pitch is hard to read, Tabloop checks whether the note you should be playing is there by its harmonics. A wrong note or one still ringing from before isn't taken for the right one.
  • Fast notes through heavy distortion are now placed on time (they used to show up a little early in your takes).

0.57.0 12 November 2026

Real guitar, bass and drum sounds

  • 🎸 HQ sounds: the tab now plays with recorded guitars (clean, distorted, nylon), basses (finger, picked) and a drum kit instead of the synth. They download once (about 1.3 MB) the first time you play, then work offline; until then the synth plays. Turn them off or remove them in ⚙ Settings → Audio.
  • Bends, slides, vibrato, hammer-ons, palm mutes, harmonics and slap / pop all still play. Steel-string acoustic parts and keys keep the synth for now.
  • The recordings are free to use (CC0) from FreePats and Virtuosity Drums; thanks to them, credited in About.

0.56.2 12 November 2026

Space always plays

  • Space plays and pauses wherever you are, even right after clicking a button (it used to press that button again, e.g. switch Song / Tab / Both). Typing, dialogs and the Looper keep their own keys.

0.56.1 11 November 2026

Solo and mute in the Mixer

  • Each part in 🎚 Mixer has M (mute) and S (solo): S plays just that part (Cmd / Ctrl-click S to add another; S again to stop), M silences it. While a part is soloed the others are dimmed, and the metronome and count-in are quiet.

0.56.0 11 November 2026

See it from the guitar

  • A few touches to the tab's sound: acoustic, clean or lightly overdriven guitar tone (from the part), a bass that plays slap and pop, darker palm mutes, two guitars spread left and right, a little room, and reworked drums.
  • The big ▶ button gets a ring: green while 👂 Live listens, red while ⏺ Rec is armed, pulsing while it records.
  • Play's Tracks page is gone for now: each part's level and on / off are in 🎚 Mixer, and the part buttons in the top bar show or hide them.

0.55.0 11 November 2026

Edit your saved loops, Live on by default

  • Change a saved loop: move its start or end and 💾 Save asks whether to Update it (and rename it if you like) or Save as new. The loop card says “edited” until you save, and a saved loop you haven't changed shows ✓ Saved.
  • 👂 Live turns on when a song opens, so every play-through is judged. Turn it off in ⚙ Settings → Playing & feedback (“Turn on 👂 Live when a song opens”). Turning Live off during a song lasts for that song.
  • In Songs' Library and Recent, 🔁 N beside a song shows its saved loops; click one to open the song and loop it.
  • Play's tabs show what's on: a blue dot on Loop while the loop is on, green on Play along while Live listens, red on Takes while Rec is on.
  • The song's tempo at your speed is shown next to the speed (e.g. 90 BPM at 75 %).

0.54.0 11 November 2026

Record again, your saved loops, a song's stats

  • Opening a song now takes you to Play.
  • Takes: a ▶ / ❚❚ on each take in the list, its own ⏺ Rec button, and ⏺ Record another next to Prev / Play take: it goes back to the loop start (with the count-in) and records. Choose whether each ▶ records One take or Keeps going, a take every pass.
  • Loop: the start and end controls each fit on one line, the card shows the loop's name (a saved loop, a section, or Custom), and this song's saved loops are now under the loop controls instead of in Sections.
  • 📊 This song on the Play along page (and in Progress → Songs): your best Live pass and when, top speed, how often and how long you played it, and your takes.
  • The version is in the browser tab's title, and shows when you hover over the logo.

0.53.0 11 November 2026

A Takes page, and a Loop switch

  • Play has a fifth page, Takes: ⏺ Rec and your takes (the take you're on, play it, previous and next) moved there from Play along. Turning on ⏺ Rec opens it, and its tab shows a dot while Rec is on.
  • The Loop page always shows the loop's controls and the speed trainer, even with no loop. ⟲ Loop: On / Off replaces ✕ Clear: On loops a few bars from where you are (like the L key), Off removes the loop and the controls stay. Changing the start or end, or ticking the speed trainer, turns the loop on.

Home

0.51.1 11 November 2026

0.51.0 11 November 2026

Play in pages, pitch next to the speed

  • Play now has pages, with tabs at the top like Songs: Loop (the loop and the speed trainer), Sections (the song's sections and its saved loops), Tracks and Play along (Live, Rec and your takes, Sing). One page at a time, no long scrolling, and it remembers the last one you used. Turning on Loop opens the Loop page; Live or Rec open Play along. On a phone the four pages are small buttons next to the cards under the tab.
  • Pitch moved to the controls under the tab, right after the speed: the button shows the pitch now (±0, +2…), and a tap opens a small panel with − and + and how the tab follows. The song keeps playing while you change it. Esc or a tap elsewhere closes it.
  • In Songs, Open file, Folder, YouTube and Playlist now come first, above the search and the list.
  • The layout button (Rail or Modes) is in the top bar on phones too, as an icon.

In Song parts it is the big pedal, in Pedal the footswitch. Shift+Space starts or stops everything, like ALL START / STOP.

0.49.0 10 November 2026

Everything in five places: Songs, Play, Train, Progress and Create

  • Songs is where you find something to play: your library, recent songs, playlists and saved loops in one list with a search, and Open file, Folder and YouTube.
  • Play has everything for the song you're playing. Controls: the loop, the speed trainer, pitch, the sections and this song's saved loops, the tracks and the mixer. Play along: Live, Rec and your takes, and Sing.
  • Train is one skill at a time: Scales, Ear training or Backing tracks. Each shows where you left off, so Continue picks it up in one tap, or start something new. The trainer then fills the screen, with its own header and a way back to Train.
  • Progress is a full page of how you're doing: today's practice session, your streak and time, a calendar you can look back on year by year, your songs, your ear training, this week's goals and target practice. Tap any of it to go there: a task opens the song at the right section with the speed trainer set.
  • Create gathers everything that makes or changes a song: edit the tab, transcribe, tab from a picture, create a song or a new empty one, sync, instruments and drums, the vocal melody, the looper and Save pack.
  • Tune & Input, in the top bar, is the one place to get your instrument ready: your input and its level, monitoring, the tuner, calibration and checking your input.
  • The view buttons above the tab now include Song / Tab / Both and Lyrics, and the controls below it (speed, metronome, count-in, Live and Rec) are there for songs, scales, backing tracks and the looper alike. A practice session's timer sits there too: tap it to pause, skip or mark a task done.
  • On a phone, the tab stays on screen while you practise: the controls for the song are a row of small cards under it (tap one for more), and turned sideways they're a panel at the side.
  • On a wide screen you can pin the sections beside the tab. The layout choice (rail or tabs) is in Settings → Display.

0.47.0 10 November 2026

The looper becomes a pedalboard

  • Pick how your looper looks and works: a multi-track station, loops for each part of a song, or a single pedal. Switch any time, even while it plays; your loops stay as they are.
  • Station: three tracks side by side (add a fourth or fifth), each with its own footswitch, a ring that lights up as its loop goes round, mute, stop, undo and level, plus one switch to start or stop them all. Keys 1 to 5 play the tracks' footswitches.
  • Make a track two or four times as long as the loop, for a longer line over a short groove; it always stays in time with the others.
  • Song parts: a loop for the verse, the chorus and the bridge (rename them, add more). Tap the next part while you play and it comes in right on the next bar line, or at the end of the loop, so you can build and play a whole song.
  • Pedal: one big footswitch and a ring for each layer. Tap to record, overdub or play, hold to undo, tap twice to stop; tap a ring to mute that layer.
  • Saving keeps it all: each track becomes its own track in the song, and song parts play one after another, each marked as a section. Your old looper sessions open in the new looper.

0.46.0 10 November 2026

A looper, like a looper pedal

  • Record a loop, then layer over it as many times as you like: set the tempo, the time signature and the length in bars, or just play and let your first layer decide how long the loop is.
  • A count-in and a click get you started, and a big bar and beat counter shows where you are in the loop.
  • Mute, solo, level or rename any layer while it plays, undo and redo layers, and use the space bar as your pedal.
  • The layers stay perfectly in time with each other, however long you keep going, and there's no click where the loop starts again.
  • Save your loop as a WAV file, or as a song with a track per layer, ready to open, slow down and write a tab over.
  • Time spent with the looper shows up in your practice stats. Find it in Play along → Looper, or on the welcome screen.

0.44.0 10 November 2026

Your practice, as you really did it

  • Practised at a lesson, a rehearsal or on an unplugged guitar? Tick off the tasks of a practice plan session, change their minutes, mark a goal as met and add a note.
  • Add a session you did on an earlier day (“yesterday, 20 minutes of scales”), and look back at every session in the new History tab.
  • Add practice time to any song for a past day. What you enter is marked as yours, counts towards your streak and calendar, and can be edited, deleted or undone. Nothing is counted twice.

0.42 9 November 2026

Backing tracks to improvise over, and practice plans

  • Backing tracks: a full band (drums, bass, keys and rhythm guitar) in the style you choose, from blues shuffle, rock and funk to jazz swing, bossa nova, reggae and metal. Pick the key, the tempo and the chords, from ready-made progressions or typed in.
  • A chord chart follows the music, showing the chord now and next, and the scales that fit.
  • Improvise and see every note you play on a fretboard: a chord tone, in the scale, or outside it. Record takes over the backing, or save the backing track as a song.
  • Practice plans give your practice a shape: ready-made plans for beginners to advanced players, or your own, fitted to your minutes a day and days a week. A timer takes you through today's session, block by block.
  • Scales: a new cascade exercise (8 7 6 5 · 7 6 5 4 …), up, down or both ways, optionally with hammer-ons and pull-offs. (0.42.1)

0.41.0 9 November 2026

Write a song from scratch, and let Tabloop write down what you play

  • Start an empty song, no recording needed: give it a tempo, time signature, key and length, add guitar, bass, keys or drum parts with their tunings, then write the tab.
  • Transcribe: play along and Tabloop writes the notes on the tab as you play, on the string and fret a player would choose, with hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, bends and vibrato. Check it, then keep it or throw it away.
  • You can transcribe a take you recorded earlier too.

0.39 9 November 2026

Scales and ear training

  • Scales: any scale in any key on the fretboard, from the major scale and its modes to pentatonics, blues, harmonic and melodic minor. See it in CAGED shapes, three notes per string, pentatonic boxes, at any position or across the whole neck, on guitar or bass in any tuning.
  • Practise a scale as a tab, at your tempo, up, down, in threes or fours, with the speed trainer and the metronome.
  • Choose how many notes per string you play, from one to six, or type your own pattern. (0.39.1)
  • Ear training: hear two notes and name the interval, or answer by playing the second note on your instrument.
  • Ear training keeps track of your progress session by session, shows which intervals are improving and which need work, and can start a round of just those. (0.39.2)
  • Time spent on scales and ear training counts in your practice stats and streak.

0.38.0 9 November 2026

Your own recordings, with a tab

  • Line the tab up with the recording by nudging it, tapping along to the beat, or letting Tabloop guess.
  • Songs you're working on are kept on this device, so you can come back to them later.
