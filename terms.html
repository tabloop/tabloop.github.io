What Tabloop is

Tabloop is a free web app for practising music. It plays song packages (.feedpak files) with their tab, and lets you slow songs down, loop parts, record yourself and track your practice. It runs entirely in your browser: there is no Tabloop server and no user account. You don't pay anything to use it, and there are no ads or purchases.
