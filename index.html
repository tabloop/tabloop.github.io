🗂 Side panels

Sections, Saved loops and Tracks (the toolbar buttons open and close them). Stacked: each open panel shows its title, and clicking a title expands that panel and folds the others up. Side by side: Sections and Saved loops share the height (drag the bar between them; double-click for half and half), with Tracks below.

📐 Layout

Portrait is for phones and tablets held upright, or a monitor turned on its side: the song gets most of the height. One slim bar at the top (the song, its parts and a ☰ menu with everything else), the side panels open over the tab, and play, seek, ⟲ Loop and speed stay at the bottom. Automatic (on by default) switches to Portrait while the screen is taller than wide and back to Standard when you turn it, so a phone or tablet held upright gets Portrait and a computer keeps Standard; turn it off to always use the Layout chosen above. Kept for this device only (not synced).

🎵 Lyrics

For a song with lyrics that you open for the first time: the karaoke bar, the In tab row and the lyrics in the 🎤 Vocals tab. Each song then remembers its own Lyrics tick in the toolbar, whatever this is set to.

🎯 Note feedback

How the notes you play are shown on the tab, with 👂 Live and recorded takes.

Markers: 🎯 nailed (on time and in tune); a triangle where a note was off, pointing to what: ▲ sharp, ▼ flat, ◀ early, ▶ late; yellow a little off (up to twice the limit), red clearly off; ✕ missed. The limits: ±40 ms and ±15 ¢. Hover a marker for the numbers.

⚖️ How forgiving the judging is

How far off a note can be and still count as right — on time within ± ms, in tune within ± cents. One setting for 👂 Live and recorded takes: the ✓ and X/Y counts, ghost colours, markers, take summaries and timing marks all follow it, and what's shown is judged again right away.

Typing a value makes it Custom (timing 10–150 ms, pitch 5–50 ¢). Now: ±40 ms and ±15 ¢.

For when you're sure of the notes and want to work on speed and timing: a note played on time counts as right whatever its pitch — only early, late, missed and extra count (no flat or sharp). Ghost notes are coloured by timing (green on time, yellow a little off, red clearly off), markers show only 🎯 ◀ ▶ ✕, and the pitch heard is still shown on the hint card (not judged). Also the ⏱ button in the 👂 Live bar (takes follow it too: their summary then says “Timing only”). Singing below is judged on pitch by nature, so it isn't affected.

🎤 Singing (🎙 Sing in the Vocals tab) has its own, wider pitch limit: each sung note counts as in tune when the middle of it is within ± cents of the melody. Timing isn't judged for singing.

Typing a value makes it Custom (5–100 ¢). Now: ±30 ¢.

⏱ Calibration

Line up what you see, hear and play on this setup: the cursor against the sound (screens and Bluetooth headphones are often late), and your input's delay, used by ⏺ Rec, 👂 Live and 🎤 Sing. Then a short check. Kept per output and input device in this browser only (never exported or synced).

🎚 Levels and the input

The song's, the tab synth's, the metronome's and My take's levels, the audio input's level and monitoring are in the 🎚 Mixer (toolbar), remembered as you set them; the input device is chosen in the record, live and vocal bars.

🔄 Sync folder

Keep settings, practice history, saved loops and playlists in sync across your computers: choose a folder on this computer that Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive syncs (e.g. a “Tabloop” folder in Google Drive), then the same folder on your other computers. The app then saves to it and reads from it automatically.

💾 Export / import

Settings, per-song state and practice history are kept in this browser. Export them to back them up or to move them to another browser.

☁ Google Drive

Optional: back up settings and practice history to your own Google Drive, in a private app folder only this app can see. With several computers, each compares itself with the backup when it starts and asks if both changed.
