🔄 Sync folder

Keep settings, practice history, saved loops and playlists in sync across your computers: choose a folder on this computer that Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive syncs (e.g. a “Tabloop” folder in Google Drive), then the same folder on your other computers. The app then saves to it and reads from it automatically.
